Solmar Heist: a complete open-world crime game in three.js. Explore an 8x8-block seeded city (Solmar Bay) at golden hour, on foot and by car. Steal any parked or traffic car, drive with arcade physics through traffic that follows the road grid, dodge pedestrians who stroll and flee gunfire, and watch the 5-star wanted system bring police cars that ram and on-foot cops that chase, shoot and bust you. Five story jobs from fixer Delia Voss — steal wheels, a timed courier run, a shakedown, a hot pursuit, and a bank-vault heist ending at the marina — plus a repeatable taxi side job, health and SMG pickups, a HUD with cash, health, wanted stars, a live clock and a minimap, synthesised WebAudio sound (engine, gunshots, sirens, jingles — no audio files), and a save in localStorage that keeps cash, jobs and position between visits. Everything — city, characters, vehicles, names, story, art, sound — is original; nothing is from Rockstar Games.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```
