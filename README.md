Solmar Heist: a complete open-world crime game in three.js. Explore an 8x8-block seeded city (Solmar Bay) at golden hour, on foot and by car, across fourteen named districts. Steal any parked or traffic car, drive with arcade physics plus NOS boost (hold Shift — feather it, a flat 3-second burn overheats the kit) and drift handling (Space), through traffic that follows the road grid, dodge pedestrians who stroll and flee gunfire, and watch the 5-star wanted system bring police cars that ram, on-foot cops that chase, shoot and bust you, and a police helicopter that orbits overhead with a sweeping spotlight at 4–5 stars and calls in fresh backup at 5 stars. Ten story jobs from fixer Delia Voss — steal wheels, a timed courier run, a shakedown, a hot pursuit, a bank-vault heist ending at the marina, a Harbor Lights smuggle run, the Bayfront Grand Prix street race, the Delia Gambit finale, a Harbor Heist crate run on the North Docks, and The Solmar Crown casino finale — plus three repeatable street-race circuits with two AI rivals each (beat both for a 1.5x prize), a repeatable taxi side job, a safehouse (heal, repair, refill NOS, save), a shop (armor, SMG ammo, NOS, shotgun, rifle), four weapons (pistol, SMG, shotgun, rifle), a four-station synth car radio (Q to change station), health and SMG pickups, a HUD with cash, health, armor, NOS, wanted stars, district, race status, a live clock and a minimap, synthesised WebAudio sound (engine, gunshots, sirens, radio, rotor, jingles — no audio files), and a save in localStorage that keeps cash, jobs and position between visits. Everything — city, characters, vehicles, names, story, art, sound — is original; nothing is from Rockstar Games.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```
